It's been a while since I've done any fieldwork, and I miss it.

I missed it before I was stuck inside. Before my bedroom became my office and my adventures became simply stepping outside my door rather than into a slightly over-packed 4WD.

Now my daily run is my adventure, and I'm thankful for every long(ish) run that gets me far enough to run along the river or through the local restored wetland. I'm thankful for the weekly shop for the chance to drive with one of my housemates and pretend (even for a moment) that we're going somewhere more exciting than the local supermarket (not that a chance to stock up on more blue cheese isn't awfully exciting).

So perhaps I'm appreciating it more. The supermarket trip, the local park, the river, the wetland. I'm appreciating the green, somewhat natural spaces near to where I currently live. I'm realising I shouldn't limit my fieldwork adventures to the spaces outside the city.
