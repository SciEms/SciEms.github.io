I missed fieldwork the moment I stepped off the red sand in South Australia in 2017 and stepped back onto the pavement in tram-filled peopled Melbourne.


I missed fieldwork before I was stuck inside in 2020. Before my bedroom became my office and my adventures became simply stepping outside my door rather than into a slightly over-packed 4WD.

Knowing this, I don't know why I didn't seek fieldwork out more. I don't know why I didn't hike more. Apart from the occasional short week of volunteering and the almost as occasional hike, I mainly kept to my urban self and surrounds.

Then 2020 and 2021 happened and I tore myself apart just to see a horizon. I walked every day to the river because I needed to see the water, needed to see the current moving past to know that life was still moving even if I wasn't.

Perhaps I needed it to appreciate it all more. The supermarket trip, the local park, the river, the wetland. I'm appreciating the green, somewhat natural spaces near to where I currently live. I'm realising I shouldn't limit my fieldwork adventures to the spaces outside the city.

Now my daily run is my adventure, and I'm thankful for every long(ish) run that gets me far enough to run along the river or through the local restored wetland. I'm thankful for the weekly shop for the chance to drive with one of my housemates and pretend (even for a moment) that we're going somewhere more exciting than the local supermarket (not that a chance to stock up on more blue cheese isn't awfully exciting).

So perhaps I'm appreciating it more.
